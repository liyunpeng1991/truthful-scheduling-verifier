CERTIFICATE REPRESENTATION
The three-machine JSONL records retain root/tree/split structure, terminal
boxes and proposed dual weights. The inputs contain no precomputed terminal bounds
or search metadata. Proposed weights are stored as explicit
nonnegative rational strings, obtained with the original replay's rational
conversion rule (denominator limit 10^8). Acceptance does not rely on that
choice being optimal: the verifier checks the weights and repairs the free
dual multiplier by an exact maximum before recomputing the upper bound.

The three-machine driver adds explicit complete-tree and closed-box checks and
bounded-memory execution. The analytic wrapper checks the stronger displayed
constants, full shape coverage and endpoint nesting in addition to the original
exact kernels. The analytic kernel exposes its exact denominator/Jacobian
metrics for those checks. The rational replay and its finite input are retained.

No numerical optimizer is needed at verification time. Search history and
precomputed success reports are not part of the embedded archive. The proposed
finite data are inputs to be checked, not assumptions of successful verification.
