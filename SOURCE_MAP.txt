SOURCE MAP

Run python3 verify_scheduling.py --extract-only readable_source to inspect
the files below. MANIFEST.json gives a SHA-256 hash for each module, input
and notation file. The launcher checks that the manifest covers the archive.
run_checks.py verifies those hashes, runs the requested groups and writes
their results to the report.

Section 6. The analytic two-machine optimum

check_analytic.py connects the interval calculations to the bounds in the
paper. analytic/analytic_exact/ contains the uniform and endpoint polynomial
data, the shape data, and replay_stable_curve_fraction.py, which bounds the
residual and the contraction. analytic/prepare_curve_shape_input.py
reconstructs the shape enclosures. The other modules in analytic/ check the
shape on its whole parameter interval, the signs at the boundary, and the
Hessian estimates.

Section 7. Three machines

check_three.py verifies the two worked examples, reconstructs and exhausts
each subdivision tree, and checks the bounds on its terminal boxes.

three/rational_cone.py computes exact simplex probabilities and the assignment
law for each clock map. three/scheduling.py evaluates the makespan of each
complete assignment. three/strata.py enumerates the support and minimum-order
patterns. three/verify_fullsupport_orbits.py checks relabeling for the two
full-support roots.

three/fullsupport_kernel.py and three/strata_kernel.py bound probabilities and
costs on full- and mixed-support boxes. three/dual_weights.py checks the
proposed multipliers. three/volume.py computes clipped box volumes as a
second check on the cover. three/data/ contains 74 files: one for the two
full-support roots and one for each of the 73 mixed-support roots.

Section 8. The piecewise rational two-machine rule

rational/upper_candidate_EXACT.json contains the knot values, coefficients,
maximizing pair and bound. rational/replay_rational_upper.py reconstructs the
pieces, checks 178,506 candidates across 44,118 polygons, and evaluates the
original objective at the maximizing pair.
